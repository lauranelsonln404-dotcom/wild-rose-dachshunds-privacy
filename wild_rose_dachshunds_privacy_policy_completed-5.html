Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 24, 2014

Last Updated: February 15, 2025

Wild Rose Dachshunds respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

1. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Mailing or delivery address
  • General location or community
  • Information about your household
  • Information relevant to determining whether a puppy may be an appropriate match for your household
  • Information about current or previous pets
  • Information about your experience with dogs
  • Information about your home, family, lifestyle, or work schedule that you voluntarily provide
  • Communications and correspondence with Wild Rose Dachshunds
  • Information relating to reservations, purchases, adoption arrangements, payments, or contracts
  • Other information you voluntarily provide to us

2. How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information when you send us a Facebook message, interact with our social media pages, complete an inquiry or adoption application, contact us by phone, text, or email, request information about a puppy, make an appointment, enter into an agreement with us, arrange pickup or delivery, or otherwise voluntarily provide information to us.

3. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for reasonable business purposes, including responding to inquiries, processing applications, communicating with prospective puppy adopters, determining whether a puppy may be an appropriate match, arranging appointments or delivery, processing reservations and transactions, preparing contracts, providing customer support, maintaining business records, preventing fraud or misuse, meeting legal obligations, protecting our rights and property, and resolving disputes.

4. Consent

By voluntarily providing personal information for an identified purpose, you may provide consent for us to collect, use, or disclose that information for that purpose where applicable. Where express consent is required, we will request it in an appropriate manner.

You may withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. If you withdraw consent, we will explain any consequences that may result.

5. Facebook and Social Media

If you contact Wild Rose Dachshunds through Facebook or another social-media platform, your communication is also subject to that platform's privacy practices and policies.

Information you choose to post publicly, including comments, may be visible to other users. Please avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly. For matters involving personal information, we may ask you to continue the conversation through a private communication channel.

6. Sharing Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose personal information when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or when permitted or required by law. Depending on the circumstances, information may be shared with service providers, payment or transaction providers, delivery or transportation providers, professional advisers, government authorities, regulators, courts, or law-enforcement agencies where appropriate.

7. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. We do not intend to store complete credit-card numbers, banking passwords, or similar payment credentials ourselves unless necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.

8. Protection of Personal Information

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, theft, unauthorized access, use, disclosure, copying, modification, or destruction.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal and regulatory obligations. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely destroy, delete, or anonymize it.

10. Access and Correction

You may request information about the personal information Wild Rose Dachshunds holds about you. You may also request correction of information that you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to an access or correction request.

11. Privacy Questions and Complaints

If you have a question, concern, or complaint about how we handle your personal information, please contact our privacy contact first. We will make reasonable efforts to investigate and respond.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or another applicable privacy regulator.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended to be arranged by adults. We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information directly from children for puppy adoption applications or transactions.

13. Photos and Testimonials

If you voluntarily provide photographs, testimonials, or other content to Wild Rose Dachshunds, we will use that content only for the purposes for which you have provided permission or where otherwise permitted by law.

14. Cookies and Website Technologies

If Wild Rose Dachshunds operates a website, the website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics, or improving the user experience. Third-party services may have their own privacy practices.

15. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our social-media pages, website, advertisements, or other content may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

16. Privacy Breaches

If we become aware of a privacy breach involving personal information under our control, we will take reasonable steps to contain the breach, assess the circumstances, and provide any notifications required by applicable law.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our business practices, technology, applicable laws, or privacy requirements. When changes are made, we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this policy.
